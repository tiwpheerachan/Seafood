## Notes for the build

- **Bilingual treatment is essential.** Show Thai and English side-by-side in the menu UI. Tourists love seeing Thai script; Thai customers need it for clarity.
- **Signature items** get visual prominence — bigger image, callout/badge treatment. The seven confirmed signatures: Seafood Tom Yum, Steamed Mussels, Steamed Crab, Spicy Shrimp Curry, Khao Yum, Massaman Curry, Deep-Fried Fish.
- **"Market price" is honest** — for whole crab and grilled shrimp, this is normal and not a red flag for tourists. Don't fabricate prices.
- **Estimated prices ranges should be replaced** with verified prices from the owner before pitching the demo. Or, on the demo only, swap the price column for a "see today's price" CTA that links to their FB Messenger.
- **Photography per category** is the most important factor. A tom yum photo with steam rising is worth more than perfect prose.
- **Spice indicator** — consider adding a chili icon next to spicy items. Tourists appreciate the warning; it's not patronizing.
