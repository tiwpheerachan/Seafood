# Customer Reviews — Sentiment & Sample Quotes

> Sourced from Google reviews (3,087 total), TripAdvisor, Wongnai, Trip.com. Use for: (1) recurring themes for marketing copy, (2) social-proof badges, (3) understanding what to emphasize on the site.

---

## Recurring praise themes (use these to drive copy)

The 3,087-review pool consistently surfaces these themes:

1. **Freshness of seafood** — "super fresh", "you can tell the catch is local", "crab tastes like the sea"
2. **The view** — "sunset is unreal", "best beachfront seats in Ao Nang", "karsts on the horizon"
3. **Generous portions** — "we couldn't finish", "huge plates", "meal for 4 was a feast"
4. **Authentic Thai flavors** — "real spicy", "tom yum done right", "tastes like a Thai grandmother made it"
5. **Value vs Ao Nang restaurants** — "cheaper than the touristy strip", "worth the tuk-tuk ride"
6. **Atmosphere over service** — service is described as "fine", "fast", "casual" — not "premium" or "polished". Set expectations honestly.

## Negative / mixed signals (for risk awareness)

Surface occasionally:
- **Service can be slow at peak times** (sunset crowd). Mitigation: "Reserve for sunset hours" CTA.
- **Cash-friendlier than card** at some times — verify current payment options before pitching.
- **Sand seating is not for everyone** — some elderly tourists find it hard to get up. Already addressed in the locations page accessibility note.
- **Closes at 9:30 PM** — earlier than some tourists expect for a beachfront restaurant. Be explicit about this in hours display.

## How to use review content on the site

- **Trust signal in the hero:** "4.8 ★ on Google · 3,000+ reviews" — display prominently, ideally near the reservation CTA.
- **Testimonial section:** 3 short paraphrased / approved quotes max. Don't pad — feels desperate.
- **Embed Google reviews widget:** Free Elfsight or Embedsocial widgets pull live Google reviews. Better than hardcoded testimonials because they update.
- **TripAdvisor badge:** If they have a Certificate of Excellence, display it. Tourists trust TripAdvisor badges more than they trust the restaurant's own claims.

## ⚠️ Copyright & ethics note

For the **demo / pitch**, use only paraphrased / summarized review sentiment. Never publish a customer's verbatim review without permission — it's both bad form and a copyright issue.

For the **production** site, the safest pattern is:
- A live-updating Google Reviews widget (the user has implicitly consented to public display by leaving a Google review)
- Plus a single owner-approved highlight quote with the customer's first name + initial

The 3,087-review count itself is the strongest social proof — far stronger than any individual quote. Lean on it.
