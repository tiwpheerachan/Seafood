No. 01 — Our table · โต๊ะของเรา

The boats land at Ao Nam Mao
in the morning. เรือเข้าฝั่งตอนเช้า · ครัวเปิดตอนเที่ยง

By midday the kitchen is moving. By sunset the long tables fill up, the karsts go pink, and nobody's in any hurry to leave.

The menu is southern Thai — deep-fried whole fish with three-flavor sauce, steamed mussels with seafood dipping sauce, tom yum with whatever was best at market, and the curries that make this part of Thailand worth flying to. Massaman. Spicy shrimp. The rice salad called Khao Yum that tastes like nowhere else.

Ra Beung Lay isn't fine dining. It isn't trying to be. It's the kind of restaurant that earns three thousand five-star reviews because it does the simple things — fresh seafood, generous portions, a view that does the rest of the work — without any of the fuss.
