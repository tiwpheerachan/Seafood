No. 04 — Questions · คำถามที่พบบ่อย

Anything else we can tell you?

Do I need to book ahead?

For sunset hours (5:00 PM onward), yes — especially high season. Lunch and post-sunset are usually walk-in-friendly. Larger groups (6+) should always book.

Is the restaurant on the actual sand?

Yes. There are three seating styles: long communal wooden tables under thatched roofs, low Thai-style platforms with cushions, and small tables right on the sand for sunset. Tell us what you'd prefer when you book.

How accessible is the venue?

The thatched-roof platform tables are the most accessible. Sand seating is not for everyone — some guests with limited mobility find it hard to get up. Ask for a platform or covered table when you book.

Do you serve alcohol?

Singha, Chang, and Leo beer on ice — yes. Coconut water, Thai iced tea, lime soda, soft drinks. Wine availability varies — call ahead if it matters to you.

How spicy is "Thai spicy" here?

Real. The tom yum is hot, sour, and fragrant in the way southern Thai food is supposed to be. Tell your server "ไม่เผ็ด" (mai phet · not spicy) or "เผ็ดน้อย" (phet noy · a little spicy) — they're used to the question.

Children?

Welcome. The space is open-air with plenty of room to move, and the staff are gentle with kids. Pad Thai, fried rice, and the tom kha gai are tourist-friendly defaults.

What about dietary restrictions?

Most dishes can be made without fish sauce or shrimp paste — ask. Stir-fried morning glory and several stir-fries are vegetarian by default. The kitchen is not a certified gluten-free space.
